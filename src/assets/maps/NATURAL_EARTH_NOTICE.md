# Natural Earth map data

The generated files in this directory are derived from Natural Earth 1:50m Cultural Vectors:

- Admin 1 – States, Provinces (version 5.1.1)
- Populated Places (version 5.1.2)

Natural Earth data is public domain. Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/

Regenerate with `tools/generate-map-gazetteer.mjs` using the two official Natural Earth ZIP archives.
