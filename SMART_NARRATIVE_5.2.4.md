# PowerPoint Smart Narrative - 5.2.4

PowerPoint export now analyzes the rows already loaded for each visual and adds concise findings beside the captured report page.

## Narrative coverage

- Bar, column, stacked, pie, donut, treemap, funnel, scatter, and bubble visuals identify the highest and lowest category.
- Pie, donut, treemap, and funnel visuals also calculate the leading category's contribution to the total.
- Line, area, and combo visuals describe the change from the first category to the last and retain high and low findings.
- Card, KPI, gauge, progress, and multi-row card visuals state their current values and target variance when available.
- Tables and matrices state the displayed row count and add high and low findings when a numeric value is bound.
- Slicers state the number of available values.
- Stacked and multi-series results aggregate repeated category rows before ranking them.

## Slide layout

- The report name and generated date remain at the top.
- The complete report-page image appears on the left.
- The Smart Narrative panel appears on the right.
- Findings are grouped by visual title and limited to short key points so the slide remains readable.
- PDF export remains unchanged.
